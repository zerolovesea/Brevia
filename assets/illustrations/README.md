# 首页插画

[voice-to-action.webp](voice-to-action.webp) 为本轮使用内置 imagegen 生成的原创透明插画，表示声音、笔记与行动项。保留透明通道，转为 WebP 用于网页加载。它用于产品概念说明，不代表应用截图。

生成提示词：

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: original transparent 3D illustration for Brevia's transcription and AI landing page, displayed in a white feature panel.
Primary request: a beautifully art-directed small composition showing the journey from spoken voice to written notes to a completed action. Three tactile sculptural objects: a compact rich cobalt-blue audio waveform with softly rounded bars, an ivory paper sheet with a few raised gray horizontal lines, and a small pale-blue rounded square with a vivid blue checkmark. The waveform floats upper left, paper sits diagonally in the center, check tile floats lower right, composed as one coherent loose diagonal group.
Style/medium: premium physically rendered 3D editorial product illustration, precise bevels, ceramic matte surfaces, subtle realistic ambient occlusion, gentle studio shadows, sophisticated and quietly playful, minimal high-end productivity website.
Composition/framing: landscape 3:2, entire object group visible with comfortable margins; gently front-facing isometric camera; large enough objects to read at 450px wide. Objects overlap slightly, no background surface, no UI frame.
Lighting/mood: large soft studio light upper left, delicate shadow depth, bright and airy.
Color palette: cobalt #0878d1, cool white, light blue, neutral gray. No purple, no pink.
Text: none. Paper lines only, no letters.
Constraints: genuine transparent background with preserved alpha, no logos, no watermark, no photographic microphone, no computer or phone, no extra objects, no borders or enclosing card.
```

## 手绘版

[conversation-sketch.webp](conversation-sketch.webp) 为内置 imagegen 生成的原创透明手绘插画，用于官网的声音、笔记与行动流程。画面使用墨线、彩铅纹理、对话气泡、笔记本、便签和铅笔；通过 cwebp 转为 WebP，保留透明通道。它用于概念说明，不代表应用界面。

生成要求：front-facing editorial notebook doodle collage, imperfect double ink contours and colored-pencil texture, cobalt blue, cream paper, warm yellow, orange and sage; speech bubbles flow into a notepad and checked sticky note; no readable text, logos, watermark, enclosing rectangle or 3D rendering; actual transparent background.
