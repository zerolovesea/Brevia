Capture · Understand · Retrieve

One quiet arc, three moving parts

Every feature follows the same path — from the moment audio arrives to the moment you go looking for it again.

01

Live transcription & translation

Open it, press record, watch the captions appear. Microphone and system audio are captured at once, so both sides of a remote call land in the same transcript. Optional live translation renders alongside the caption stream.

02

AI meeting notes in one pass

When a meeting ends, plug in any LLM provider and Brevia drafts the summary, key decisions, and action items together. Run a bundled model on your own machine, or connect Claude, OpenAI, or OpenRouter. Only text is sent, never audio.

03

Voiceprints that carry across meetings

Enroll a short voice sample per teammate and Brevia recognizes them by name in every future meeting — not "Speaker 1, Speaker 2," but the people they are. Recognition works across recordings, so finding what someone said last week is a single click.

04

Floating caption actions

Keep captions visible at the bottom of your screen while you work in other apps. When you need to revisit a line, click to copy, translate, play, or jump to its place in the timeline—without returning to the app window.

05

Rich model library

Choose and download the capabilities you need for captions, translation, speaker identification, and meeting notes. Pick a lighter setup for speed or a more complete one for better results—no technical vocabulary required.

Privacy

What happens on your machine stays on your machine

Speech recognition, diarization, and speaker embeddings all run on-device. Recordings, transcripts, and voice profiles are never uploaded. When you opt into AI notes, only the transcript text is sent to the provider you choose — never the audio.

No telemetry, no tracking

The renderer opens no network ports. No usage data is collected, and there's no analytics SDK.

Your data stays yours

Recordings, transcripts, exports, voiceprints, and the SQLite database live in ~/brevia by default.

Cloud calls are opt-in

Text is sent only after you configure a provider yourself, and every IPC message is validated.

Auditable source

Fully open source under the ISC license — the privacy claims can be read line by line, not just taken on faith.

Questions

Does any audio leave my device?

No. Speech recognition and speaker processing run locally. If you opt into AI notes, only the transcript text is sent to the LLM provider you configure — the audio never leaves your machine.

Which AI providers can I use?

A bundled model runs entirely on your machine, or you can connect Claude, OpenAI, OpenRouter, or any service that speaks the OpenAI or Anthropic chat format.

How are voiceprint samples stored?

Voice embeddings (a small float vector) and reference audio live in a local SQLite database and on your filesystem. Nothing leaves the device, and deleting a profile removes the associated data.

What platforms are supported?

macOS on Apple Silicon and Windows on x64. On first launch, grant microphone and screen-recording permissions, then download the models you need from the Model Library.

Can I use Brevia without an internet connection?

Yes. Live captions, recording, transcription, and local models run on your device. An internet connection is only needed when you choose an external AI service for meeting notes.

What meetings and audio can I capture?

Brevia records microphone and computer audio together, so it works with common meeting apps, audio and video in your browser, and in-person conversations. Capturing computer audio requires screen-recording permission the first time.

Do I need to download every model?

No. Download models by language and capability as you need them. Start with a lightweight setup for everyday meetings, then add translation, speaker identification, or higher-quality models later.

Can I export my meeting record?

Yes. Export transcripts and notes as Markdown, TXT, JSON, SRT, DOCX, or PDF; export audio as FLAC, WAV, or M4A.
