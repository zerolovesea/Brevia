Brevia 隐私政策
更新日期：2026-10-16

提供者与范围

Brevia 由 YANG ZHOU 提供。手机录音和本地回看无需账号。应用不含广告或行为分析 SDK，不会自动向开发者上传会议。Brevia 不提供在线 AI 代理或统一 AI 账号；在线 AI 地址与账号均由用户自行配置。

录音、转写与同步

录音、笔记、逐字稿和会议设置保存在你的设备上。离线录音只有在你选择电脑并确认上传后才同步；选择实时转写或将手机用作电脑麦克风时，音频及必要的会议资料会发送到已配对电脑，语音识别在电脑执行。配对和同步需要手机与电脑处于同一局域网；首次配对还需电脑确认，配对本身不会上传已有离线录音。

可选在线 AI

本地 AI 在电脑运行。选择在线 AI 后，电脑可能发送会议标题、逐字稿（含说话人和时间点）、笔记及 AI 上下文，用于生成纪要和实时 AI 笔记；这些 AI 请求不发送原始录音。请在电脑配置中核对服务商、地址和模型；启用在线 AI 或生成在线纪要后，相关文字会按该配置发送。不启用在线 AI 仍可使用录音、本地转写和本地 AI。关闭实时 AI 可停止后续自动请求，已发出的请求无法撤回。服务商可能按其政策保留请求、日志及生成结果；请在配置和使用前核对其隐私政策，并直接向所选服务管理或删除数据。自备 API Key 不代表服务商不会保留数据。

连接安全

手机与电脑仅通过局域网 HTTPS 通信，并校验已配对电脑的证书指纹。

权限与更新检查

麦克风用于录音和试音，相机仅用于扫码配对，局域网权限用于发现和连接电脑。手动配对可替代扫码。权限可在系统设置撤销；来电、强制停止或系统限制可能中断录音。在 iOS 主动检查更新时，应用向 Apple 查询公开 App ID 与商店地区，不发送会议内容；Apple 可接触请求的网络信息。

保存、删除与导出

手机配对凭据存入系统安全存储；iOS 录音目录使用系统文件保护并排除应用配置的系统备份。会议保留至你删除或卸载应用。删除手机副本不会删除电脑副本，取消配对不会删除已保存的会议，也不会删除第三方已收到的数据。卸载前请先导出或同步尚未上传的录音。自行创建的备份、导出文件及在线 AI 服务上的数据需分别管理。

支持与政策变更

支持和隐私咨询：https://github.com/zerolovesea/Brevia/issues 。反馈由你主动提供；公开反馈中不要包含录音、凭据或个人信息。请在录音前取得参与者同意。功能或数据处理方式变化时，我们会更新本政策及日期。政策网页：https://brevia.work/privacy.html

Brevia Privacy Policy
Updated: 2026-10-16

Provider and scope

Brevia is provided by YANG ZHOU. Phone recording and local review require no account. The app has no advertising or behavioral analytics SDKs and does not automatically upload meetings to the developer. Brevia provides no online AI proxy or shared AI account. Users configure their own AI endpoints and accounts.

Recording, transcription and sync

Audio, notes, transcripts and meeting settings are stored on your devices. Offline recordings sync only after you select a computer and confirm upload. Live transcription or using the phone as a computer microphone sends audio and necessary meeting information to the paired computer, where speech recognition runs. Pairing and sync require the phone and computer to be on the same local network; initial pairing also requires computer approval. Pairing alone does not upload existing offline recordings.

Optional online AI

Local AI runs on the computer. With online AI selected, the computer may send the meeting title, transcript (including speakers and timestamps), notes and AI context to generate summaries and live AI notes. These AI requests do not send original audio. Review the provider, endpoint and model in the computer settings. Enabling online AI or generating an online summary sends relevant text according to that configuration. Recording, local transcription and local AI remain available without online AI. Turning off live AI stops future automatic requests; requests already sent cannot be recalled. Providers may retain requests, logs and results under their policies. Review the selected provider’s privacy policy before setup and use, and manage or delete data with that provider. Using your own API key does not mean the provider retains no data.

Connection security

Phone and computer communicate only over local network HTTPS connections, which verify the paired computer’s certificate fingerprint.

Permissions and update checks

Microphone access records audio and checks input levels; the camera only scans pairing codes; local network access discovers and connects to computers. Manual pairing can replace scanning. Permissions can be revoked in system settings. Calls, force-stop and system limits may interrupt recording. When you check for updates on iOS, the app sends Apple the public App ID and store region, not meeting content. Apple can access network information associated with the request.

Storage, deletion and exports

Phone pairing credentials use system secure storage. The iOS recording directory uses system file protection and is excluded from system backup by the app. Meetings remain until you delete them or uninstall the app. Deleting a phone copy does not delete the computer copy. Unpairing does not delete saved meetings or data already received by third parties. Export or finish syncing pending recordings before uninstalling. Manage your own backups, exports and data held by online AI services separately.

Support and policy updates

Support and privacy enquiries: https://github.com/zerolovesea/Brevia/issues . Feedback is voluntary; do not include recordings, credentials or personal information in public issues. Obtain participants’ consent before recording. We will update this policy and its date when features or data practices change. Policy website: https://brevia.work/privacy.html
