## Why Brevia

For online calls, in-person discussions, interviews and lectures. Keep the full conversation, your own thoughts and the next steps together.

- **Keep recording within reach.** Reuse the latest meeting setup for quick recording, then control recording and review captions and notes through the menu bar, tray or floating capsule while the main window is minimized or hidden.
- **Correct text in bulk.** Preview and select replacements in transcripts, notes and summaries, with undo. During recording, apply rules to subsequent captions in the current meeting.
- **Stay with the conversation.** Follow live transcripts in 30+ languages, with optional translation and floating captions.
- **Keep what matters.** Write beside the transcript, let AI suggest decisions and action items, and edit notes and summaries in your own words.
- **Take the meeting room with you.** Record and mark moments on your phone, then continue on your computer. Your phone can also serve as its microphone.
- **Keep control of your recordings.** No account required. Speech recognition runs on your computer, with offline processing and your choice of optional online AI.

## Get started

1. **Prepare Brevia.** Install the desktop app and download a speech model for your meeting language in **Settings → Model Library**.
2. **Capture the conversation.** Start a meeting with microphone audio, system audio or both, or import an existing recording.
3. **Leave with clear next steps.** Take notes alongside the transcript, edit the meeting summary, then export and share decisions and action items.

For phone setup and pairing, see the [mobile guide](../mobile/README.md).

## From conversation to action

### Listen closely. Keep a complete record.

Live transcripts and notes sit side by side, so you can check the original words as you write. Add your own thoughts while AI helps surface decisions, action items and open questions. You choose which suggestions to keep.

### Keep recording within reach

The floating capsule keeps recording status, elapsed time and pause controls at hand. Expand it for live captions and translation, meeting notes and AI suggestions. Pin the panel open or retry a failed save, then click the Brevia icon to return to your meeting.

![Floating recording capsule: captions, notes, pause and save states](assets/showcase/desktop/en/capsule.gif)

### Turn the meeting into a plan

Turn a long discussion into key points, decisions and next steps. Your summary stays editable: add owners, check the details and share it with the team. The transcript and recording remain together whenever you need the original context.

![Editable meeting summary with decisions and action items](assets/showcase/desktop/en/summary.gif)

### Find the conversation you need

Group meetings by project or team, then search titles, transcripts, speakers and tags to revisit a discussion. Post-meeting refinement can identify speakers for a clearer record. Export text and notes as **Markdown, TXT, JSON, SRT, DOCX or PDF**, and audio as **WAV**.

![Meeting library and workspace navigation](assets/showcase/desktop/en/library.gif)

Available in English, Simplified Chinese, Spanish, Japanese, Korean, French, German and Russian.

## Record on your phone

Capture conversations and ideas at an interview, a lecture or a meeting room, then let your computer do the organizing.

| Situation          | Workflow                                                                                                                           |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Capture on the go  | Record offline, write notes and mark key moments. Upload to your computer for transcription later.                                 |
| Read as you record | Start a meeting on your phone. Your computer on the same LAN transcribes and returns text and notes.                               |
| Record a room      | Place your phone near the conversation as the computer’s microphone. Control recording and follow the transcript on your computer. |

## Your recordings, on your devices

- **Process locally.** Speech recognition and speaker processing run on your computer. With the required models downloaded, AI notes, summaries and translation can work offline too.
- **Connect locally.** Phone and computer communicate over LAN HTTPS. Brevia operates no meeting cloud or remote relay and does not automatically upload meetings to the developer.
- **Choose your AI.** If you select an online provider, relevant transcript and note text is sent to that service. AI requests do not send raw recordings. See the [privacy policy](https://brevia.work/privacy.html).

## Architecture

- **Desktop:** Electron and JavaScript, with HTML and Tailwind CSS for the interface.
- **Local processing:** A Python worker coordinates audio and models; MLX / sherpa-onnx handle speech inference, and llama.cpp runs local AI.
- **Mobile:** Flutter and Dart share the interface and recording workflow across iOS and Android.
- **Storage and sync:** SQLite stores desktop meeting records; phones and computers sync over LAN HTTPS.

## FAQ

### Can I use Brevia without the internet?

Yes. Desktop transcription and local AI work after the required models are downloaded. Phone-only recording needs neither a computer nor an account; phone transcription needs your paired computer, which can be reached over the local network without internet access. Online AI needs internet access.

### Which models should I download?

Start with the suggested speech model for your language. Add a local AI model for notes and summaries, and a translation model if needed. The Model Library shows download sizes; choose a smaller local AI model on a less powerful computer. You do not need every model.

### Where are my recordings stored?

Desktop data defaults to `~/brevia`; model and recording folders can be selected during setup and changed in Settings. Phone recordings and cached text are stored in the app. Deleting the phone copy does not delete the computer copy. Export or sync pending recordings before uninstalling the phone app, as uninstalling removes its local data.

### Windows shows a SmartScreen warning

Confirm that the installer came from the official [release page](https://github.com/zerolovesea/Brevia/releases), then choose **More info → Run anyway** if you trust the download.

### Why won't my phone connect to the computer?

Make sure both devices share a reachable LAN, the computer is awake with Brevia open, and local-network access and TCP port 43187 are allowed. Guest Wi-Fi isolation, VPNs and sleep can prevent a connection. If scanning is unavailable, enter the address and PIN shown on the computer. See the [mobile guide](../mobile/README.md) for more details.
