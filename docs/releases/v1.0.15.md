# Brevia v1.0.15

## Highlights

- Added workspaces for organizing meetings, with workspace switching, assignment, and localized labels.
- The live meeting panel can now collapse into a right-side drawer.
- Meeting details now use the available workspace width and include refined transcripts.

## Fixes

- Large meetings no longer fail to open when their worker response exceeds the previous output limit.
- Improved localization for workspace actions and refreshed the Chinese and Spanish documentation.
- Removed source separation and the dual-track recording view from the product interface.

- **macOS (Apple Silicon):** `Brevia-1.0.15-arm64.dmg`.
- **Windows x64:** `Brevia-1.0.15-x64-setup.exe`.
