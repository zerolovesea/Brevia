# Brevia v0.1.7

Brevia v0.1.7 improves AI meeting notes, post-meeting transcription quality, and desktop audio handling.

## Highlights

- Reworked AI meeting notes into a two-pass workflow that first cleans the transcript and then generates structured Markdown, with visible progress for cleanup, generation, and saving.
- Added richer AI-note controls: local Ollama and Ollama Cloud support, provider-aware configuration, regeneration, and Markdown, TXT, or PDF export.
- Fixed post-meeting speaker diarization so automatic clustering converges on stable voice groups instead of creating a new speaker for short fragments; adjacent sub-second speaker flips are absorbed into surrounding turns.
- Improved refined transcripts with speaker-aligned recognition windows, overlap-aware word timestamps, overlap trimming, duplicate suppression, and preservation of user-edited or locked-speaker segments.
- Prevented macOS system-audio cleanup from stopping an already-ended loopback stream, and added a close button that stops and resets background playback.
- Expanded and refreshed the English, Chinese, Spanish, Japanese, Korean, French, German, and Russian documentation, including install, build, architecture, and troubleshooting guidance.

## Data compatibility

Existing meetings, recordings, model downloads, configurations, summaries, and voice profiles remain in the same local Brevia data directory.

## Installation notes

These are unsigned development builds. macOS may require **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**. Windows may show a Microsoft Defender SmartScreen prompt; choose **More info → Run anyway** only after verifying the download came from this release.
