# Brevia v2.1.0

新增会议术语库，按业务议题整理长会议纪要与原话依据，更新手机工作台，并修复纪要核对与笔记保存问题。

Add a meeting glossary, organize long meeting minutes and source quotes by topic, refresh the mobile workspace, and fix minutes verification and note saving.

## 新功能 / What’s new

- 新增工作区与通用术语库，可确认 AI 发现的名词、补充释义与替换词，在纪要中查看释义或恢复原词，保留原始逐字稿。<br>Add workspace and shared glossaries to confirm AI-suggested terms, add definitions and replacements, and view definitions or restore original wording in minutes while preserving the source transcript.
- 较长的中英文会议使用内置模型或 DeepSeek V4 时，可按业务议题整理纪要与原因解释，每条内容核对对应原话。其他语言、自定义提示与其他服务保留七章纪要。<br>Longer Chinese and English meetings using a built-in model or DeepSeek V4 can produce thematic minutes and cause explanations, with each item checked against its source speech. Other languages, custom prompts and other providers retain seven-section minutes.

## 体验改进 / Improvements

- 纪要分批展示可读初稿与原因图，核证完成后补齐引用并保存；取消或失败时保留原有纪要。<br>Preview readable minutes and cause diagrams as sections arrive, then add verified references and save. Cancellation or failure preserves the existing minutes.
- 更新手机会议库、底部导航、录音准备、扫码配对及笔记阅读布局，桌面设备页与官网同步展示当前手机界面。<br>Refresh the mobile library, bottom navigation, recording setup, QR pairing and note layouts, and update the phone previews in the desktop app and website.

## 问题修复 / Fixes

- 修复模型校对漏项或重复返回时静默丢失纪要内容的问题，补齐数字与纯问句的来源校验。<br>Prevent silently dropped minutes when model verification omits or duplicates items, and check numbers and question-only sources.
- 修复术语替换影响 Markdown 代码、链接与导出格式，以及保存中重复提交或旧请求覆盖新草稿的问题；术语界面统一支持八种语言。<br>Keep glossary replacements from altering Markdown code, links and export formatting, prevent duplicate saves and stale requests from replacing new drafts, and localize the glossary in all eight interface languages.
- 手机笔记在切到后台时立即触发保存，减少最后输入尚未落盘的风险。<br>Save phone notes immediately when the app enters the background to reduce the risk of losing the latest edits.
