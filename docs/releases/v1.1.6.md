# Brevia v1.1.6

## New

- Added a unified export and sharing hub for transcripts, meeting notes, personal notes, and recordings, including ZIP bundles for multiple selections.
- Meeting notes now support inline images, and edited AI meeting notes can be saved.

## Improvements

- Added automatic audio-source selection: system audio stays in low-power standby until sound is detected, while microphone capture continues normally.
- Improved meeting search, live-caption throughput, and refinement performance; lower-powered devices now prioritize real-time captions.

## Fixes

- Fixed Windows audio import and upload compatibility issues.
