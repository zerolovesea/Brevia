# Brevia v1.1.8

## New

- Copy a meeting summary directly from the meeting details view.
- Exported files now include a content-type prefix, making transcripts, meeting notes, personal notes, and recordings easier to distinguish.

## Improvements

- Audio resampling now keeps a continuous timestamp across capture blocks, improving timing accuracy in long recordings.
- Meeting summaries more reliably preserve explicitly stated follow-up actions, including actions without an assigned owner or due date.

## Fixes

- Removed the unused summary-dialog editor; summary editing now consistently happens inline in the meeting details view.
